Common questions

I bought a cosmetic on another device / reinstalled — how do I get it back?
Open Cosmetics in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they restore for free.

Does Knave cost money to play?
No. The game is free with no ads and no pay-to-win. The only purchases are optional cosmetic themes and card backs that change how the game looks, never how it plays.

Do I need an account?
No. Knave has no login. Your progress is saved on your device.
