Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

Knave (“the app”) is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your high scores, unlocked content, settings, and selected cosmetics are stored only on your device (in standard iOS storage). We never receive or transmit this information, and it is not linked to your identity.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. To manage and restore purchases we use RevenueCat, which receives your purchase transactions and an anonymous identifier it generates for the app. This does not include your name, email, or other contact details, and is not used to track you or for advertising. See Apple’s and RevenueCat’s privacy policies for how they handle payment and purchase data.

Analytics & tracking

Knave does not use any advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not collect a device advertising identifier and we do not share data with data brokers.

Children

Knave does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
